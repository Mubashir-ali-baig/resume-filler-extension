Privacy Policy for Resume Filler Extension

Last updated: January 15, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Resume Filler ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Chrome extension.

1. Information We Collect

Resume Filler collects the following information to provide our services:

  • Email address: Used for user authentication and account management
  • Password: Stored securely in hashed format (never stored in plain text)
  • Resume files and text: Uploaded by users to generate personalized interview answers
  • Cover letter files and text: Uploaded by users for personalized answer generation
  • Executive summary: User-provided text about their professional background
  • Profile data: Stored to personalize AI-generated responses
  • Authentication tokens: Stored locally in Chrome storage to maintain your login session

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain your login session
  • Generate AI-powered interview answers based on your resume, cover letter, and job descriptions
  • Store your profile information for future use
  • Provide personalized assistance with job application forms
  • Improve our services (using anonymized, aggregated data only)

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the following locations:

  • Supabase: User profiles, resume files, cover letter files, and authentication data are stored securely in Supabase's cloud database and storage
  • Chrome Local Storage: Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage API
  • Backend API: Processes AI requests temporarily (data is not permanently stored on the backend)

4. Data Security

We take security seriously and implement the following measures:

  • Passwords are hashed using secure algorithms (bcrypt) before storage - we never see or store your plain text password
  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • API endpoints are secured with authentication tokens
  • Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensure users can only access their own data
  • Authentication tokens expire automatically for security

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide our functionality:

  • Supabase: Database and file storage service. Their privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Backend API: Hosted on Vercel for AI processing and data management. Vercel's privacy policy: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • AI Services: We use AI services (via our backend) to generate interview answers. No user data is shared directly with AI providers - all processing happens through our secure backend

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data:

  • With service providers (Supabase, Vercel) necessary to provide our services
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With your explicit consent

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can access your personal data through the extension interface
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us or using the extension's sign-out feature
  • Correction: You can update your profile information at any time through the extension
  • Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data by contacting us

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of the extension after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
